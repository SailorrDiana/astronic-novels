# Astronic Novels

**A visual novel creator to bring your stories to life.**

Astronic Novels is a web-based tool for creating and editing visual novel scenes. Arrange backgrounds, characters, and dialogue in one place and turn your ideas into scenes ready to preview and export.

> [Versão em português](README.md)

---

## ✦ Features

- **Projects:** create, save, open, and delete projects.
- **Scenes:** organize multiple scenes within a single project.
- **Backgrounds:** upload your own background images.
- **Characters:** add sprites and arrange them in left, center, and right positions.
- **Compositions:** place multiple characters in the same position and remove them individually.
- **Customization:** adjust character size and height.
- **Dialogue:** customize character names, text, fonts, font sizes, and colors.
- **Preview mode:** test your scenes in an interactive format, advancing and going back through dialogue.
- **Export:** export your scenes as PNG images.

---

## ✦ How to use

1. Open Astronic Novels in your browser.
2. Create a new project and choose a name.
3. Add a background to your scene.
4. Upload character sprites and arrange their positions.
5. Write and customize the dialogue.
6. Create additional scenes as needed.
7. Save your project to preserve your progress.
8. Use preview mode to test your scenes or export them as PNG images.

**Important:** save your project after making changes. Do not assume that every modification is saved automatically.

---

## ✦ Storage and privacy

Projects are stored locally in the user's browser, using the storage available on their device.

- Projects are not sent to an Astronic Novels server for storage.
- The creator of Astronic Novels does not have remote access to users' projects.
- There is no automatic synchronization between devices or browsers.
- Projects are associated with the browser and website origin used to create them.

**Please note:** clearing browser or website data may permanently delete locally stored projects. We recommend keeping backups whenever possible.

Local browser storage does not provide an absolute guarantee of security or confidentiality, especially on shared devices.
